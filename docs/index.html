Resonance works fully without an account. This page explains exactly what we collect if you choose to sign in, why, and how to remove it.

01 Information we collect

Information you enter

  • Birth date, time, and place — used to calculate your natal chart. Stored on your device, and in our database if you sign in.
  • Birth details for anyone you save for a compatibility reading — handled the same way as your own.
  • Mood and body check-ins (energy, sleep, resting heart rate, and similar) that you log yourself.
  • Your practice history — sessions completed, journal entries, saved preferences.

Account information

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and Google account ID, used only to identify your account and keep your data in sync. We never request or see your Google password.

Location

If you grant permission, Resonance reads your device's location to calculate local sunrise/sunset times and refine your daily reading. This is entirely optional — the app works fully without it. Location is stored on your device and, if you're signed in, in your account. It is never shared with anyone else.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Resonance Pro, Google Play handles your payment directly — we never see or store your card details. Our subscription-management partner, RevenueCat, receives your purchase and subscription status (not your payment details) so the app knows your entitlement is active.

What we don't collect

No analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers — there simply isn't a pipe for it to travel through.

02 How we use your information

To calculate your readings, personalize your daily practice, remember your preferences, keep your data backed up if you sign in, process your subscription, and respond if you contact us. Nothing more.

03 Where your data lives

  • On your device, always — the primary copy of your data, whether or not you sign in.
  • In the cloud, only if you sign in — hosted on Supabase, with row-level security so only your own account can read your own data.
  • Payments run through Google Play Billing; subscription status through RevenueCat. Neither shares your data with us beyond what's described above.

04 Notifications

Every notification — your daily reading, Moon phases, void-of-course alerts — is computed and scheduled entirely on your device from astronomical calculations. Nothing is sent to a server to generate them.

05 Your choices

  • Use the app with no account at all — your data stays on your device only.
  • Sign in to back it up — undo this anytime by signing out.
  • Delete your account and all associated cloud data anytime, from Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.

06 Children's privacy

Resonance is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

07 Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections and row-level access control — to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable, considered steps to protect what you share with us.

08 Changes & contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the effective date above whenever we do; continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the update.
