## Notes for the rest of the listing

- **Category:** Lifestyle (primary). Astrology apps live here, not Health & Fitness.
- **Tags:** astrology, horoscope, birth chart, tarot, meditation, breathwork, mindfulness, moon phases, numerology-adjacent — pick from Play's fixed tag list; astrology + horoscope + meditation are the high-value ones.
- **Feature graphic (1024×500):** the ResonanceMark on midnight with the tagline "The sky, read for you".
- **Screenshots (order matters — first 2–3 are seen in search):**
  1. Today screen — the mantra + "Today's Practice" card
  2. Full daily horoscope (long-form)
  3. Birth chart wheel
  4. The Chakra Field
  5. Tarot spread (3-card)
  6. The Runes daily
  7. Journal — aura + streak milestones
  8. Compatibility / synastry
  Caption each with a 3–5 word benefit line.
- **Do not** mention a free trial anywhere — there is none.
- Prices are intentionally left vague in the description; Play shows the real localised price in the subscription section.
